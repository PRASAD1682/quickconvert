Privacy Policy

In short: to convert your files, they are uploaded to our server, processed, and then automatically and permanently deleted within 1 hour. We do not read, share, sell, or keep your files.

1. What happens to files you upload

When you convert a PDF or image, it is sent to our server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, converted, and made available for you to download. A background cleanup process deletes both the uploaded file and the converted output within 1 hour, whether or not you downloaded it.

2. Do you look at my files?

No person reviews uploaded files. They exist only long enough to be converted and briefly held for download.

3. Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense (or a similar network) to help cover server costs. These networks may use cookies to show relevant ads; you can control ad personalization through Google's Ads Settings.

4. Server logs

Like most web servers, ours may keep basic technical logs (timestamps, IP address, error messages) for a short period to diagnose problems and prevent abuse — not tied to the content of your files.
